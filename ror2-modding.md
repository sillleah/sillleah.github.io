Here is my collection of modding tutorials for Risk of Rain 2.  
Please be aware, these tutorials are a HUGE work in progress.
To be frank, I find the existing documentation in the community to be painfully lacking and convoluted, and I am still in the process of learning to mod this game myself - so large portions of these tutorials are prone to change as I become a more experienced modder.
Please feel free to reach out to me on Discord if you have questions or issues.

If you aren't sure where to start, I'd recommend working through these tutorials in the order they're listed below. Once you have the basics down, don't be afraid to jump around to focus on things that interest you most.
